Customization

This is the guide on how to customize your new NFC tag!

Step 1

Install the NFC Tools app

Free on App Store and Google Play. Turn on NFC in your phone settings (Android). iPhone 7 and up read NFC with no setup.

Step 2

Copy your link

Instagram profile, Spotify playlist, Linktree, portfolio, résumé PDF. One link only; use a Linktree-style page if you want several.

Step 3

Write > Add a record > URL

In NFC Tools, open the Write tab, tap Add a record, choose URL / URI, paste your link, tap OK.

Step 4

Tap Write, hold the keychain to your phone

Back of the phone, near the top camera. Hold still until it says Write complete. Takes about two seconds.

Step 5

Test it

Lock your phone, tap the keychain to it. Your link should pop up. Change your mind later? Repeat steps 2 to 4 anytime.
